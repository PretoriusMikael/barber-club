### How a project runs

Four steps from a first hello to a site that keeps working, and nothing starts until the price is in writing. 1. A short call: fifteen minutes on a call or on WhatsApp about what the business does and what the site has to do. 2. A written quote: a fixed price and a start date, in writing, before any work begins; half books the date. 3. The build, with you: you see it take shape and say what should change, in rounds of review, before anything goes live. 4. Launch, then Care: it goes live on your own domain and the other half is due; Care keeps it hosted, updated and changing. Your domain is in your own name from the start, half is paid at launch and not before, and everything is handed over if you ever leave.

Page: https://elevate-web.co.za/#how

He works in Next.js, React and TypeScript, with GSAP for motion, and pays close attention to how a site feels on a phone on mobile data. He quotes in South African rand.
